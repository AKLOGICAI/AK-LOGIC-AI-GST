# AK-LOGIC AI GST — Mobile Application

Official Android mobile companion application for **AK-LOGIC AI GST**. Designed with Google Material 3 design system and fully aligned with the Senior Web App architecture.

## Modules & Workflows
- **Live Self-Billing QR Code**: Customer scan-to-bill workflow (`AKM-000125`)
- **Billing Requests Management**: Review, live Intra/Inter-state GST calculation, approve & reject
- **Invoices & AKAI Historical Audits**: Dual invoice numbering (`INV/...` & `AKM-...`), PDF download, WhatsApp share, parcel delivery dispatch
- **Deep Accounting**: 15 Standard Chart of Accounts, balanced books indicator (`Diff: ₹0.00`), customer receivables & supplier payables
- **Smart Purchase Bills**: OCR scanning, Input Tax Credit (ITC) reconciliation, automated inventory restocking & storefront sync
- **Smart Inventory**: Multi-category stock tracking with SKU, HSN, Cost Price, Selling Price, and online catalog status
- **Merchant B2B Network**: Verified local supplier directory & broadcast stock requirement requests
- **Online Storefront Builder**: Theme switcher, catalog integration & live mobile preview (`shop.ak-logicai.in/store/AKM-000125`)
- **Reports & GST Return Center**: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B filing summaries & analytics
- **Recharge & Plans**: Tiered plans catalog & custom branding policy
- **@AKAI AI Assistant**: Business intelligence and automated live audit controller
